## v1.0.1

- The PC helper checks for updates too and tells you from the tray when a new version is out. Nothing installs without your OK.

## First release

- Remote for VLC on your PC: play, pause, fullscreen, seek, skips, volume, mute, subtitles
- Movies screen: browse a folder on your PC and play from the couch
- Mouse screen: touchpad, scroll strip, click buttons and a keyboard for your PC
- One-time pairing with the BingeMote PC helper (BingeMoteHelper.exe below)
- Checks for updates each time it opens; nothing installs without your OK

Android 12+. See the README for the PC setup.
