## v1.0.1

- Setup is now a real step-by-step flow: one step on screen at a time, with Back working on every step
- The "helper is running" button actually moves to the next step
- If the phone paired before, setup offers to continue with that PC instead of forcing a new code, and "Start over" lets you re-pair anytime
- The pair screen explains how to get a fresh code from the helper's tray icon

## First release

- Remote for VLC on your PC: play, pause, fullscreen, seek, skips, volume, mute, subtitles
- Movies screen: browse a folder on your PC and play from the couch
- Mouse screen: touchpad, scroll strip, click buttons and a keyboard for your PC
- One-time pairing with the BingeMote PC helper
- Checks for app updates each time it opens; nothing installs without your OK

Android 12+. See the README for the PC setup.
