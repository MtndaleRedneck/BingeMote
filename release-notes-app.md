## v1.2.0

- Movies now play even when VLC is closed: BingeMote PC finds the movie and starts it for you (needs BingeMote PC 1.2.0)
- Two phones can control the same PC - each phone pairs once with its own code from the BingeMote PC window
- The PC program has a new name, BingeMote PC, so setup and messages say what to look for

## v1.1.0

- The Movies tab is locked to your folder, rows respond to touch, and the d-pad scrolls the list; the folder picker in Settings browses the PC's folders with a Set button
- Volume buttons match the approved design: speaker with sound waves (one for down, three for up)
- The big play/pause button flips instantly on tap
- Skip buttons do a full spin per tap
- New pointer-speed slider in Settings
- Launcher icon: the couch no longer gets cut off, and no black corners on square-icon phones

## v1.0.1

- Setup is now a real step-by-step flow: one step on screen at a time, with Back working on every step
- The "helper is running" button actually moves to the next step
- If the phone paired before, setup offers to continue with that PC instead of forcing a new code, and "Start over" lets you re-pair anytime
- The pair screen explains how to get a fresh code from the helper's tray icon

## First release

- Remote for VLC on your PC: play, pause, fullscreen, seek, skips, volume, mute, subtitles
- Movies screen: browse a folder on your PC and play from the couch
- Mouse screen: touchpad, scroll strip, click buttons and a keyboard for your PC
- One-time pairing with the BingeMote PC helper
- Checks for app updates each time it opens; nothing installs without your OK

Android 12+. See the README for the PC setup.
